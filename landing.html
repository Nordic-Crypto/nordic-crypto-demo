FAQ

Questions?
Answered.

Everything you wanted to know about NordicCrypto.

What is NordicCrypto?
NordicCrypto is an all-in-one payment and crypto banking app. Fund your balance, manage digital assets, use your Visa card, and handle payments — in a few clicks.
Are there any fees for registration?
Signing up is free. Virtual cards start at $5. Physical metal cards ship from $100. Card payments 1.5%, ATM 1.5% + $1. Crypto top-up is free.
How do I get the card?
Open the app, complete a quick verification (up to 5 minutes), top up your balance and activate your virtual card instantly. Physical card ships from Stockholm in 5 days.
How can I fund my account?
Stablecoins (USDT, USDC) on Tron, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Solana. Fiat transfers: SEPA (EUR), SWIFT (USD), ACH, and more — depending on your region.
How do I withdraw my funds?
Tap Withdraw in the app — send to an external crypto wallet instantly. Bank withdrawals via SEPA are available for verified accounts.
How do I contact support?
In-app chat (Elena Bergström) — replies within 2 minutes. Or email: support@nordiccrypto.net
