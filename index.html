    Questions, answered.

    What is NordicCrypto?

    An app that combines a card, an IBAN and a crypto exchange. You fund your balance, hold assets and pay for things from one place.

    How do I get a card?

    Open the app, finish a quick verification (up to 5 minutes), top up, and your virtual card is ready. A metal card ships from Stockholm in about 5 days.

    How can I add funds?

    Send stablecoins (USDT or USDC) on Tron, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon or Solana. Fiat options include SEPA (EUR), SWIFT (USD) and ACH, depending on your region.

    How do I withdraw?

    Tap Withdraw to send crypto to an external wallet. Bank withdrawals by SEPA are open to verified accounts.

    Is my money safe?

    All transfers are protected with two-factor authentication, device-bound sessions, and rate limiting. Every transaction is shown with full details before you confirm.

    How do I contact support?

    In-app chat, or email support@nordiccrypto.net — we usually reply within 2 minutes.
